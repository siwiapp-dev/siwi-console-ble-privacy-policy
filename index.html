Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the SiWi Console BLE Terminal app (hereby referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by SiWi (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, transmit, or share any personal user information to external servers. Your information is safe with us, and we respect your privacy.

However, the Application requires certain device permissions to function properly for its intended industrial and commercial use (connecting to and configuring SiWi hardware):

  • Bluetooth and Nearby Devices: The Application requires Bluetooth permissions to scan for, connect to, and communicate with nearby hardware devices (such as printers, sensors, or controllers). This is the core functionality of the Terminal.
  • Location Permission: Android OS requires the Application to request location permission to perform Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scans. We do not track, collect, or store your actual geographic location. This permission is strictly used to discover nearby Bluetooth devices.
  • Storage/File Access: The Application may request access to local storage solely to allow you to save terminal logs, export text files, or load firmware/configuration files to the connected hardware. This data remains locally on your device.

2. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, no personal data is retained by the Service Provider. Any terminal logs, app settings, or serial commands are stored locally on your device and can be cleared by the user or by uninstalling the Application.

3. Third-Party Access

The Application does not transmit data to third parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track your personal behavior.

4. Children's Privacy

This Application is intended for professional, technical, and industrial use. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
